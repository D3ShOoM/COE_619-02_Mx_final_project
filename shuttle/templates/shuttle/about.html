What this Django demo implements

A software-first prototype for a privacy-preserving campus shuttle information system.

Implemented demo capabilities

  • Django backend: models for routes, stops, buses, GPS pings, occupancy readings, segment stats, and events.
  • Real-time location: two simulated buses with API-refresh and optional continuous management command simulator.
  • GTFS-RT-style feeds: JSON VehiclePositions and TripUpdates endpoints for easy inspection in demos.
  • ETA: segment travel-time estimates with a scalar Kalman update helper.
  • Occupancy: Low/Medium/High levels from counts and capacity thresholds with hysteresis.
  • Privacy: no cameras, no audio, no phone/device identifiers; only aggregate count and vehicle location data.
  • Operator dashboard: last-seen, feed freshness, occupancy, pings, and logs.

Next integration points

Replace the seeded coordinates with final stops, connect GNSS hardware to /api/ingest/gps/, connect the dual-IR count logic to /api/ingest/occupancy/, and switch SQLite to PostgreSQL/Timescale for longer trials.
